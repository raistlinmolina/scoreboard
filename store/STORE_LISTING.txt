FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)
Moli-Puck is a clean, easy-to-read scoreboard for inline and roller hockey,
designed to be run from a tablet or Chromebook at the rink.

Features:
• Large, clearly visible game clock — tap to start/stop, with a big START/STOP
  button and quick +/- time adjustments.
• Configurable periods (count and length).
• Big, bold team scores with simple +/- controls; a goal horn for the home team.
• Penalties per player number with configurable preset durations (e.g. 1:30,
  4:00, 10:00) or a custom time; they count down while the clock runs and clear
  automatically or on demand.
• Configurable team names and logos.
• Adjustable clock and score sizes so it reads well on any display.
• Loud horn, auto-sounded at the end of a period.
• Landscape, full-screen, and keeps the screen awake during a game.

Works fully offline. No accounts, no ads, no data collection.

Concept, design and development by José Manuel Molina.
