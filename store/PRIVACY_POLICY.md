# Privacy Policy — Moli-Puck

_Last updated: 2026-10-07_

Moli-Puck ("the app") is a hockey scoreboard application developed by
José Manuel Molina.

## Data collection

Moli-Puck does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app
works fully offline. The only data it stores is your own configuration (team
names, team logo image you choose, period/penalty settings, display
preferences), which is saved **locally on your device** and never leaves it.

- No analytics.
- No advertising.
- No accounts or sign-in.
- No network transmission of user data.

If you select a team logo image, it is read from your device and stored only on
your device for display in the app.

## Permissions

- **Photos/Media (optional):** only used if you choose a team logo image from
  your device. The app does not access your media otherwise.

## Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect any data
from anyone, including children.
